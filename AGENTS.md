# Instructions for agents

This file is for coding agents that edit the landing page. Read `README.md`
first for how the site is built and how the copy is translated.

## Do not bring back the stock landing-page patterns

Commit `4fd675b` removed the patterns that make a page look generated by an AI
tool. Do not add them again, in English or in French.

1. Do not use Inter or a system default as the main typeface. Headings use
   Bricolage Grotesque and body text uses Instrument Sans. Caveat writes
   the notes in the margin (`.eyebrow`, `.note`, the lead review) and
   nothing longer. All three are self-hosted in `assets/fonts/` and declared
   at the top of `assets/styles.css`. Do not load fonts from a third-party
   server.
2. Do not put a rounded badge or pill above the hero headline. If something
   is news, put it in the headline.
3. Do not put a grid, dot or line pattern behind the hero.
4. Do not show a numbered "1, 2, 3" walkthrough. The "How it works" section
   is two unequal panels (`.split`): the organiser's short part and the
   group's longer part.
5. Do not put three identical cards in a row. The reviews use one large lead
   quote (`.review--lead`) and two smaller quotes beside it. When you add a
   set of items, vary their size or their count.
6. Do not use the em dash (U+2014) in the copy, the HTML, the CSS or the French
   dictionary. Use a comma, a colon, parentheses or two sentences.
7. Do not bring back the SaaS surface: no frosted header, no gradient text,
   no glowing radial blobs, no browser-window dots on the mockups, no pill
   buttons with a coloured glow. The page is paper and ink.
8. Do not write slogans in threes, such as "Free, open source, offline".
   Say each fact once, as a plain sentence.

## Before you finish

1. Search the files that you changed for the em dash:
   `grep -n '—' index.html 404.html assets/styles.css assets/i18n/fr.js`.
2. Make sure that every `data-i18n` key in `index.html` has a French entry in
   `assets/i18n/fr.js`, and that `fr.js` has no unused keys.
3. Serve the site with `python3 -m http.server 4173`. Look at it in English
   and in French, at desktop width and at phone width.
4. Do not write facts about the product or its author that are not in the
   repository.
