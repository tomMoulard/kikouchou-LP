You have rented a house with friends. Eleven people, six rooms, arrivals spread over four days and three trains to meet at the station. Kikouchou gets all of that out of one person’s head and onto a plan everyone can see and fill in themselves.

How it works

You spend two minutes on it. The group does the rest.

You, the organiser

Set it up once

  • Create the trip. Name, dates, and the rooms with their beds.
  • Paste the link in the group chat. That is your last logistics message.

Everyone else, on their own phone

They fill in their own part

  • Claim a bed for the nights they are there.
  • Add their train or the time they drive in.
  • Ask for a lift from the station, or offer one.
  • Log what they paid for the groceries or the wood.

Good to know

  • Your trip is stored on your own device. An account only when you edit together, on European servers.
